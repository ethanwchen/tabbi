# Tabbi UX research, October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-08.
Goal: make Tabbi feel like the most delightful, cozy and simple notch app on the Mac.
This builds on [ui-ux.md](ui-ux.md) (HIG, Dynamic Island rules, a first teardown of notch and study apps) and [motion.md](motion.md); it does not repeat them.
It adds a second, more concrete look at the products people love, an audit of the current snapshots against them, and a prioritized list of changes.
Every finding cites the page it came from; anything not verified on a fetched page is marked "(unverified)".

## 1. What the loved apps do

Sources were fetched on 2026-10-08.
The NotchNook product page returned 404 and the MacStories Alcove review is paywalled, so those two rely on press reviews and the Alcove landing page.

### 1.1 Notch apps

- NotchNook acknowledges hover first: the notch "lightly pulses outward, with a drop shadow", and a click or two-finger swipe opens the full nook ([Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/computing/articles/macbook-notch-redeemed-130056005.html)).
- Its open "expands before gently bouncing back into place", and on pointer exit it "quickly returns to its former shape", so close is faster than open (same source).
- The same reviewer disliked reordering widgets by "repeatedly clicking arrows" and wanted drag and drop (same source).
- While music plays, NotchNook's closed notch shows album artwork and a waveform on each side ([MacStories](https://www.macstories.net/reviews/notchnook-and-mediamate-two-apps-to-add-a-dynamic-island-to-the-mac/)).
- MacStories criticized the music preview covering fullscreen content and a fake notch on external displays looking "visually jarring" (same source).
- Alcove sells its feel in a few words: "Fluid transitions", "Live Activities", "Swipe gestures", "Customizable HUDs" ([tryalcove.com](https://tryalcove.com)).
- boring.notch 2.8 adds a setting for the opening animation speed and gathers sizing, hover, gestures, animation and window behavior into one Notch settings section ([release notes](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch/releases/tag/v2.8-rc.0)).
- Its settings are one section per feature (Notch, Appearance, Media, Calendar, Shelf, and so on), and a new closed-notch indicator shows activity without opening (same source).
- Neither the boring.notch README nor its recent releases mention a Reduce Motion or minimal mode ([README](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch)).

### 1.2 Raycast

- Return runs the primary action, shown first, and Cmd-K lists the rest ([manual](https://manual.raycast.com/action-panel)).
- A bottom action bar shows the title on the left and actions with their shortcuts on the right, so people learn shortcuts by seeing them ([blog](https://www.raycast.com/blog/a-fresh-look-and-feel)).
- The stated principles are "fast, simple, and delightful" (same source).
- An empty view has a title, an optional line saying why it is empty, a centered icon and its own actions, and it is never shown while still loading ([API docs](https://developers.raycast.com/api-reference/user-interface/list)).
- Settings are a handful of tabs with power options under Advanced ([manual](https://manual.raycast.com/settings)).

### 1.3 Things 3

- About 95% of the interface is neutral, and color only carries meaning: yellow for Today, red for deadlines, indigo for This Evening ([design study](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/things)).
- Completing a to-do takes about 500 ms: the circle fills, a check scales in, a soft sound plays, then the row slides away and the gap closes (same source, the author's frame analysis).
- It uses a round completion control and a 4pt spacing base ([design system notes](https://www.oppadu.com/tools/design-systems-site/brand/things.html)).
- Today merges calendar events with to-dos and keeps This Evening apart ([culturedcode.com](https://culturedcode.com/things/features/)).

### 1.4 Session

- After a session it asks what you learned, and it tracks what you worked on and for how long ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/session-pomodoro-focus-timer/id1521432881)).
- Its copy says "rest is as important as work", giving breaks the same standing as focus (same source).
- It blocks distracting apps and sites during a session and restores them afterward ([stayinsession.com](https://www.stayinsession.com)).

### 1.5 Focus Friend and Finch

- Focus Friend's bean knits while you focus, and drops its needles if you pick up the phone ([Good Good Good](https://www.goodgoodgood.co/articles/hank-green-focus-friend)).
- Finished sessions earn socks that buy room decor, and the copy stays whimsical ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/18/hank-greens-focus-friend-app-is-climbing-the-app-store-charts-and-its-extremely-cute)).
- The home screen is one big "Focus!" button over the bean's animated room, in a coloring-book style, with a break after each session ([Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/apps/articles/bean-helped-stop-doomscrolling-phone-163017623.html)).
- In Finch, skipping a goal costs nothing; the app only offers an optional reflection ([blog post](https://chiltonm.bearblog.dev/everyone-i-know-is-addicted-to-the-bird-app/)).
- Finch reviewers stress that there is "no negative aspect" and they are never made to feel bad ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/finch-self-care-pet/id1528595748)).

### 1.6 Dynamic Island

- Compact content should be snug against the sensor and "no wider than it needs to be", with shortened units to keep the two sides balanced ([WWDC23 session 10194](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10194/)).
- Keep things in the same place between compact and expanded, so opening feels like growth rather than a page change (same source).
- Use the numeric content transition for changing numbers, and when a list reorders, move one row and fade the rest (same source).
- To get attention, expand the island briefly instead of sending a notification (same source).
- The HIG asks for medium weight or heavier text, few interactive controls (ideally one), and animations under 2 seconds ([HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-activities)).
- Apple publishes no spring parameters for the morph.

### 1.7 Patterns that carry over to Tabbi

- A gentle pet that has its own quiet activity during focus and never scolds (Focus Friend, Finch).
- A short, satisfying completion beat for checklist items (Things).
- Snug, balanced wings with shortened units (WWDC23).
- Elements keep their place when the notch opens (WWDC23).
- Hover acknowledged with a small pulse, open with a soft settle, close faster (NotchNook).
- One primary action per panel (Raycast).
- Color only where it means something, on a mostly neutral surface (Things).
- Empty states with a title, one line of why and one action, and never while loading (Raycast).
- A short optional reflection and a break offer after a session (Session).

## 2. Audit of Tabbi today

Snapshots rendered on 2026-10-08 with `TABBI_DEMO=1 swift run Tabbi --snapshot` and `swift run Tabbi --snapshot`, Midnight theme, regular, compact and large panels.

### 2.1 What already works

- The closed notch shows one glyph and one value per side (`closed-focus.png`: the timer glyph and "15:12" in the Timer accent), which is exactly the Dynamic Island compact rule.
- Every module has one accent, and the tab bar tints only the selected tab, so the black notch stays calm.
- Live empty states are designed, not blank: Now Playing offers "Open Spotify" and "Open Music", Today offers "Connect calendar", Anki says "Looking for Anki...".
- Open and close motion already uses shared springs (`Theme.Motion`), and Reduce Motion is respected in the notch, the tab bar and the celebrations.
- System, Claude Usage and Anki read at a glance: one big number per card, secondary text in grey.

### 2.2 What feels off

- Durations are written five different ways across panels, so the app reads as several apps glued together.
  Timer says "1h 4m of 2h", Today's goal says "120 min left", Up next says "in 1 h 34 min", Schedule says "1 h 40 min free", Claude Usage says "resets in 2h 15m".
  At least seven separate formatters produce them (`StudyTimerFormat.studied`, `DayReview.duration`, `ScheduleFormat.duration`, `SchedulePlanner.hours`, `UpcomingEventFormat.badge`, `PartyRoster.duration`, `ClaudeUsageFormat.duration`).
- The Timer panel has the most controls of any panel: a method picker, an info button, a Deep focus pill, seven sound buttons, a stats row, Pause, Skip and Restart.
  That is about thirteen targets in a 500x150pt canvas, against one primary action.
  The sound row repeats what the Focus tab and Settings already offer.
- The Today checklist cuts its last row in half above "Add a task" (`open-planner.png`), which looks like a rendering bug rather than "there is more".
- Ask Claude shows an answer card whose top edge is cut by the header with no fade, and four round buttons beside the field (send, history, new chat, expand).
- Empty, loading and error states never use the cat, although the pet is Tabbi's most charming asset.
  The pet appears only on the Timer dial and in the coach bubbles.
- Settings > General ends with a card that holds only a "Reset to Defaults" button on its right, which reads as an empty row, and the keyboard hint under the shortcut is right-aligned over two lines.
- The Focus panel's right card is half empty above "Focusing on", so the task floats in the middle of a large surface.
- The panel header names the module on the right ("Timer", "Today") while the selected tab already shows it; this is fine, but its weight competes with the content title in Today ("Thu, Oct 8").

## 3. Prioritized changes

Ranked by how much they make Tabbi feel calm, cozy and simple, divided by how risky they are.

1. One duration style everywhere, from one formatter in `TabbiKitCore` with tests: "45 min" under an hour, "1h 15m" and "2h" above, and "in 12 min" or "in 1h 15m" for countdowns.
   Small, testable, and every panel gains consistency at once.
2. Fewer controls on the Timer panel: keep the dial, the method, the stats line and the transport buttons; move the seven sound buttons behind one sound button that opens the mix (the Focus tab and Settings keep the full picker).
3. Today's checklist ends on a whole row or fades out at its bottom edge, like the Closet wardrobe, so a cut row never looks broken.
4. Empty and error states with the cat: the shared `StatusMessage` gets an optional small pet in place of the symbol, so "No music app is open" or "Looking for Anki..." feel like Tabbi, not like a system alert.
   Copy stays one friendly line plus one action, in the voice of Things and Finch (see section 1).
5. Ask Claude: fade the scrolled answer under the header and fold history and new chat into fewer buttons.
6. Settings > General: put Reset to Defaults and Quit Tabbi in one quiet footer row, and left-align the keyboard hint.
7. Focus panel: size the task card to its content, so the task sits near the top and the empty space goes away.
8. Closed-notch wings: keep the one glyph and one value rule for every ticker item, and check every `closed-*` shot for a value that is wider than the other side.
9. Motion: keep the existing springs, and verify the open panel grows out of the closed notch shape (no pop at the end of close, the most common notch app complaint in [ui-ux.md](ui-ux.md#31-notch-apps)).
10. Onboarding: keep it to the steps that matter for the chosen kit, and let the cat greet the user on the first screen.

Each change ships as its own small step, with snapshots rendered before and after and checked against [the audit checklist](../design/guidelines.md#audit-checklist).
