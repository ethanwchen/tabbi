I just shipped a Mac app, and it lives in the notch.

It's called Tabbi.
I made it because I study on a laptop, and my timer and to-do list kept getting lost behind other windows.
The notch is always right there, so I put them in it.

Click the notch and it opens into a small panel of tabs: a focus timer, your tasks and next meeting, music controls, Anki, and Claude.
There's also a little pixel cat that cheers you on and earns outfits as you study.

A few things I cared about while building it:

- Keep it simple. Fewer settings, good defaults.
- Keep it private. No account, no ads, no tracking.
- Keep it open. The code is free on GitHub for anyone to read or build on.

It works on macOS 14 or later, and Macs without a notch get a small virtual one.

If you try it, I'd love to hear what you think.
And if you know a student who's always fighting for focus, please send it their way.
