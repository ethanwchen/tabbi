## Voice

Ethan writes warm, simple and short.
First name only.
Plain words, no hype, no "revolutionary" or "game changer".
Say "notch" or "laptop notch", not "MacBook".
No emojis and no em dashes, in every post and every reply.

## Facts to keep consistent

- Name: Tabbi.
- Pitch: A little cat for your laptop notch.
- Price: free, open source (MIT).
- Platform: macOS 14 Sonoma or later. A Mac without a notch gets a small virtual one.
- Website: https://tabbinotch.com
- Code: https://github.com/ethanwchen/tabbi
