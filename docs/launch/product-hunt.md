## First comment from the maker

Hi everyone, I'm Ethan.

I made Tabbi because I wanted my study tools in one cozy spot, right where I already look: the notch.
I kept losing my timer behind windows and forgetting what was next on my calendar.
So I put them up top.

Click the notch and it opens into a small panel of tabs:

- A focus timer with Pomodoro and other study methods, focus sounds and Do Not Disturb.
- Today: your to-do list, your next meeting, and Plan my day, which fits your tasks into the free time you have.
- Now Playing for Spotify and Apple Music.
- Ask Claude, through your own `claude` CLI, so Tabbi never touches your credentials.
- Anki cards due today, and a Party tab to study with friends.

And there is a pixel cat.
It sits beside the notch, cheers you on while you focus, and earns outfits from your study points.

Tabbi is free and open source.
No account, no ads, no analytics.
It only connects to the internet when a tab needs to (album art, or the friends server while Party is on).

It needs macOS 14 or later.
If your Mac has no notch, you get a small virtual one.

I would love to hear what you think, and which tab you would want next.
Thank you for trying it.

## Replies to have ready

**Is it really free?**
Yes, and the code is on GitHub under MIT.
If it helps you, a star or a coffee means a lot: https://buymeacoffee.com/ethanpolar

**Does it work on a Mac without a notch?**
Yes.
Tabbi draws a small virtual notch at the top of the screen.

**Do I need Claude?**
No.
Only the Claude tabs use it, and they stay quiet until the `claude` command is installed.

**Does it slow my Mac down?**
It is a small native app with no web views, and tabs only refresh while you can see them.
