# QA log

What the whole-app quality pass found and fixed, newest first, one sentence per line.

- The resource gate holds on release builds of both editions (numbers in `docs/qa-final.md`): 25-66 MB in steady state, flat over 30 idle minutes and over 66 tab opens, 0.04% of a core idle with the notch closed, no Tabbi leaks and no network in demo runs; still open is a two-second, release-only rise of about 250 MB of graphics memory that sometimes comes when the pet coach's window first appears beside the notch.
- After main's pet growth merged, the late arrivals check out in snapshots for English and Arabic (mirrored): the Closet's bond row ("Bond 8 · Kindred spirits" with its ring) and the newest journal line fit the Compact, Regular and Large panels, the collars and sparkle draw on every breed and stance, the adventure leaves a paw print with "exploring" and the pet trots back from the notch facing home, the night doze shows "zzz" with its "z" inside the bar, the gift line names the adventure, and the closed-notch pet stays inside the 32 pt bar in every state.
- The Timer tab's method line ("25 min focus, 5 min break"), the method names in its picker and in first-run setup, and the evidence badges stayed English in every other language, because they come from `study-methods.json`; they are now looked up in the catalog by their English text, as seasonal events are, in all 12 languages, and Arabic writes the 52 / 17 method as "52/17", since a right-to-left line showed the spaced name as "17 / 52".
- The App Store build's Settings work in every pane under a throwaway sandboxed profile: General's panel size switches the open notch between Compact, Regular and Large at once, More options opens its Display, Privacy and Live activity rows, Tabs lists the kit and each tab with its Options, Look restyles the open panel (Sakura) and Reset to Default puts Midnight back, Connections removes a saved API key from the Keychain and shows the empty key field, and About shows the version with live support, privacy and terms links.
- The App Store build's Sign in with Apple starts as it should: Sign In asks for the birth month and year once, then shows the Sign in with Apple sheet, whose button (the web flow, since an ad-hoc build has no Sign in with Apple entitlement) brings up macOS's "wants to use appleid.apple.com to Sign In" consent and then Apple's own "Tabbi Web Sign In" page, with Settings reading "Signing in..."; closing that page puts the account row back to its Sign in with Apple button with no error, and the sandbox log showed nothing from Tabbi's code beyond the throwaway build's widget group and one `syspolicy.exec` lookup as the browser opened (the native sheet needs the App Store provisioning profile, so it was not run).
- The App Store build's weekly recap shows once: with a finished week of logged focus seeded into a throwaway profile, launch built the recap and posted its notification, the next notch open filled the panel with "Your week" (3h 45m focused, 9 sessions, Thursday as the best day, the bars on the right days), and neither a reopen nor a quit and relaunch brought it back, the Closet with its heatmap showing instead.
- The App Store build's AI consent works: Settings > Connections offers only the Claude, OpenAI and Gemini APIs and Ollama, picking Claude API asks "Send your AI requests to Anthropic?" with what each feature sends, Cancel leaves None in place, Allow picks it and shows the key field, a pasted key saves to the sandboxed Keychain under the build's own service, and Ask AI with an invalid key answers "Claude API did not accept the key. Check it in Settings." with a Retry button, with no sandbox denial from Tabbi.

- The pet's end-of-session moment ("6 minutes in the bank", the points and the bar toward the next unlock) appeared beside the closed notch even while the notch was open, so the open panel covered the pet and half the bubble and the user saw only "...n the bank"; when a panel is open the line now waits and the pet walks out with it as the notch closes (within 10 minutes, like the waiting crown), which a sandboxed App Store build confirms after a Stop from the open Focus tab.
- With both the Timer and Focus tabs on, Today's timer card read "Start a timer, opens the Timer tab" while a Pomodoro started in Focus was running, because Today always mirrored only the Timer tab's clock; it now shows the running Pomodoro with its pause, skip and stop controls and goes back to the Timer card once it is stopped (the demo layout, without the Focus tab, still shows the Timer card).
- The App Store build's timer went through a full focus and break under a throwaway profile (phase lengths shortened in its saved timer): the focus rolled into the break with "1 session today", the break ended idle, the heatmap took the minutes, and Stop paid the minutes focused so far (6 points for 6 minutes, none for a 90-second focus, as the 5-minute minimum says), with no sandbox denial from Tabbi's own code.
- The App Store edition, which has no Party, still told a new user in first-run setup that "Friends see it in Party" under the name question, and its About pane offered to email the team about "a person in Party"; both now leave Party out in editions without it (as Settings > General already did), in all 12 languages.
- The Essentials kit card in first-run setup promised "music, and Claude" although the tab is Ask AI and works with any AI the user picks (the App Store edition offers no Claude Code at all); it now reads "music, and an AI helper".
- The "What does your day look like?" tiles in first-run setup set their symbols at two heights, lower on one-line answers ("Lots of meetings") than on two-line ones ("Classes and homework"); every label now keeps room for two lines, so the symbols line up.
- A sandboxed App Store build (`scripts/release-appstore.sh --adhoc`, re-signed under a throwaway bundle id so it had its own container and no data of the maintainer's) went through first-run setup with every tab it ships turned on (name, kit, question, tabs, pet, Anki, calendar access granted in the sandbox, timer method, extras), opened the Closet with its heatmap, Today's yesterday view and Now Playing (Connecting while macOS asked for Automation access, then Spotify's track within 1.5 seconds of allowing it), and kept the name, pet and tabs across a quit and relaunch; the sandbox log showed no denial from Tabbi's own code (only the throwaway build's widget group, an NVMe query from a system framework and `syspolicy.exec` lookups while the Automation prompt was up, which stop once it is answered).
- A brand-new user with Spotify or Music open got macOS's "wants access to control Spotify" prompt over the first onboarding step, before anything explained it, and anyone with Now Playing turned off was asked too, because the Now Playing controller read the running players as soon as the app built it, whether or not its tab was on; it now reads and follows the players only while the tab is on and a kit has been chosen, and lets go of them (the music wings go away) when the tab is turned off, which fresh-profile builds launched with `open` confirm: the old build asked during onboarding, the fixed one asks only after setup ends with Now Playing on.
- An ad-hoc signed release build (`scripts/release.sh --adhoc`, what contributors and CI make) froze on its first launch with no notch and no onboarding, because the desktop widget's state was written to the App Group container on the main thread and macOS holds that open until the user answers its "access data from other apps" prompt (Developer ID builds are not asked); the file is now read and written on a background queue, so a pending prompt leaves the notch, onboarding and the update check running, which a sample of the relaunched build confirms.
- The Sparkle update path works end to end: an ad-hoc release build (0.1.0, build 117) of a throwaway edition with its own bundle id and data folder, pointed at a local appcast signed with a throwaway key, offered 0.1.1 (build 118), downloaded it, replaced itself in place at the same path with a valid signature and relaunched, and then turned away a 0.1.2 signed with another key ("The update is improperly signed and could not be validated") and stayed on build 118.
- App Store compliance: every web address the app ships answers (tabbinotch.com's support, privacy, terms and suggest pages, the Worker, the AI key pages, the Spotify and Apple Music focus playlists by title, not just status), the App Store edition sells nothing (the Closet and streak freezes spend earned points only) and leaves out Party, AI Usage and crash reporting as `docs/appstore.md` says; its App Privacy answer sheet named only Spotify's artwork host, so it now names SoundCloud's `i1.sndcdn.com` too, as the Privacy Policy already did.
- Known risk, left to the maintainer: every tabbinotch.com page, including the support and privacy pages the App Store listing and the app link to, has a "Buy me a coffee" link in its footer (and the home page a coffee card), which an App Review reading of guideline 3.1.1 can treat as a call to an outside payment; dropping it from the footer of those pages, or for visitors from the app, removes the question.
- Checked with nothing to fix: every Med School kit snapshot (188 shots, including the coach, Cat wisdom, gifts, Closet sections, heatmap, recap, SoundCloud and the yesterday and tomorrow views) and the Closet and recap in all eight themes look right; the backend's 395 tests and typecheck, the site build and its 14 tests pass; the Worker rate-limits every public and signed-in route and logs no tokens or codes; and the App Store release binary holds no keys, tokens or local source paths.
- Today and the closed notch showed the Anki goal half in English in every other language ("Anki reviews" with "128 cards باقي" in Arabic) and read "1 cards" in English, because Anki shared a raw title and unit; both are now catalog strings in all 12 languages, the card count is a real plural, and a bundled Arabic build reads "مراجعات Anki" and "باقي 128 بطاقة" (the `--snapshot --locale ar` renders keep back and forward chevrons pointing left to right, which the bundled app mirrors correctly, so check direction in the bundled app).
- With the System tab open the app's memory footprint rose from about 30 MB to 279 MB, because its three sparklines used Swift Charts, which kept about 225 MB of graphics memory alive; they are now plain shapes on the same monotone curve, so the open System tab holds 29 MB and uses 0.28% of a core instead of 0.94%, and `scripts/measure-performance.sh --open` now adds a tab the demo layout leaves out (it silently measured the first tab before, which hid this).
- Merging main's UX pass made its `DurationFormat` ("45 min", "1h 15m") the one duration format, so the shared goal format above now reads "2h left" and "1h 15m left" everywhere, and the Timer tab's goal label switches both sides to the hours form once either reaches an hour ("0m of 4h", "1h 4m of 2h"), so it still never mixes two styles; the Focus tab's daily tally also leaves out a focus stopped early with main's new Stop button, which earns its minutes but is no finished session.
- The Focus tab's "3 sessions done" counted every Pomodoro ever finished, because the count is saved with the timer and never starts over (so weeks in it read "137 sessions done"); it now counts today's finished focus sessions from the activity log and reads "2 sessions today", starting over each day.
- A long two-finger swipe across the open notch could land back on the tab it started from (or skip on with momentum), because after its first step the swipe handler flipped between the previous and next tab on every further scroll event; a swipe now moves exactly one tab however long it runs, momentum scrolling after it moves nothing, and a mouse's horizontal wheel steps once per stretch of travel, checked by sending synthetic gestures to a running demo build.
- A running Timer or Focus countdown started a progress ring spring every second for a move of a fraction of a point (1/1500 of a turn in a 25 minute Pomodoro), which kept the notch redrawing for nothing; the ring now sets moves under half a percent of a turn without a spring (bigger moves still glide), cutting the open Timer and Focus tabs from about 8% to about 7% of a core in back-to-back debug runs, and the rest is the deliberate rolling countdown digits.
- The System tab kept the notch redrawing every frame while it was open (about 35% of a core in a release build, the monitor showing up in its own CPU graph), because each once-a-second reading started a spring on the sparklines, core strip and memory bar and rolled the digits; readings now update without animation and the open System tab uses about 2%.
- In demo mode (`TABBI_DEMO=1`) the Timer and Focus countdowns sat still ("17:57" for as long as the panel stayed open) although they read as running, because both stores skipped their once-a-second tick in demo runs as well as snapshot runs; demo clocks now count down while their panel shows, snapshot renders still keep the clock still, and Study no longer chimes during a snapshot render.
- The Timer tab mixed two time styles in one label ("0 min of 4h", "45 min of 2h") and read the daily focus goal differently from Today and the closed notch ("1h 4m" against "1 h 4 min"); it now uses the shared goal format ("1 h 4 min of 4 h"), which still fits at the Compact panel size.
- Every `swift test` run left about 40 empty preference files (`TabbiTests.<UUID>.plist`, `KitSwitchUndoTests.<UUID>.plist` and more) in the user's real ~/Library/Preferences, because tests opened named `UserDefaults` suites, which keep their plist even after the domain is removed, and over 30,000 had piled up; every test now uses `InMemoryDefaults`, which also keeps named domains (an old build's preferences) in memory.
- A live snapshot run (`--snapshot`) had Claude Usage scan the user's Claude Code transcripts and save its scan index into Tabbi's data folder; snapshot runs now read the transcripts without saving an index, and can never probe the `claude` CLI for limits (which spends a sliver of the user's usage).
- A live snapshot run (`--snapshot`) created today's Today list file in the user's data folder (carrying yesterday's unfinished tasks over at render time), although snapshot runs must leave no trace; Today now shows the same list in a snapshot without writing it, which a run with an empty home folder confirms.
