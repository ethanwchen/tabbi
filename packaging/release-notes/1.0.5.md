### New

- **Goals in the Closet:** 12 badges, each with Bronze, Silver, Gold and Starlight tiers, for focus time, study days, streaks, sessions, flashcards, tasks, break moves, Party, study methods, steady weeks, water and monthly quests. A badge never goes away once you earn it, and breaking a streak costs nothing.
- **16 mastery outfits** that cannot be bought: a wizard's robe, a spacesuit, cozy pajamas, a tiny lantern and more, each unlocked by a badge.
- **Monthly quests:** three gentle goals each month. Missing a month costs nothing.
- Your whole history counts, so long-time studiers unlock badges and outfits right away.
