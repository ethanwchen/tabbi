### New

- **15 new outfits:** a Strawberry Cap, Mushroom Cap, Potted Cactus, Sunflower Sun Hat, Aviator Cap, Librarian Cardigan, Varsity Jacket, Sailor Collar, Daisy Chain and more.
- **The Heirloom shelf:** six long-term treasures at the top of the shop, from the Keeper's Peacoat to the Koi Yukata, the Sushi Chef, the Snow Day Puffer and floating Fireflies. Each one takes weeks of steady study.
- **Study Level:** endless levels from your focus hours, with a new rank and its emblem every 10 levels and a new name color every 5 levels up to Level 60. Your level and name color show to friends in Party.
- **Study Crews:** start or join a crew of up to 20 in the Party tab and study together. Each week the top 3 crews by focus hours win a laurel and a medal for their crest, the crews' pets take the podium, and the Hall of Fame keeps every medal. Design your crest from frames, ten cute emblems and ten colors, invite friends with a link, or ask to join a public crew. Crews follow Party's age check, and every crew can be reported.
