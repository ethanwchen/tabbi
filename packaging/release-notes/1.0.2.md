### New

- **Break Buddy:** at each break, your pet does a short move with you: a stretch, a sip of water, an eye rest, a slow breath or a stand-up. Skip it any time, nothing counts against you. Pick your moves in Settings > Pet Coach.
- **Ten new themes:** the Sweet set brings Strawberry Milk, Blueberry Milk, Deep Sea, Cherry Soda, Taro, Stargazer, Matcha Latte, Banana Milk, Mint Choco and Cotton Candy. Themes now color the timer ring, buttons and chips too, and Sakura is finally pink.
- **British Longhair:** a fluffy new cat breed, chocolate with copper eyes, in every Closet color.
- Theme names are now in your language.

### Better

- **Lighter on memory:** a song change no longer makes Tabbi's memory jump, Settings lets go of its memory when you close it, and focus sounds start without a hitch.
- **Dark pets stand out:** black, tuxedo, chocolate and other dark coats get a soft light edge, so they are easy to see on every theme.

Tabbi is free. Optional extras may come later, and the core will stay free.
