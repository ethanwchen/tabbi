### Better

- **Sign in without a birthday:** syncing your pet with your Apple Account no longer asks for your birth month and year. Party still asks once, before you join a party.
- **Clearer permission steps:** before macOS asks for your calendar, notifications or music apps, Tabbi says why and lets you pick Continue or Not now. Saying no is fine: Today and Now Playing show a gentle note with a shortcut to System Settings.

### Fixed

- Sign in with Apple could leave its window unchanged when something went wrong. Tabbi now shows the problem right in the Account row.

Tabbi is free. Optional extras may come later, and the core will stay free.
